Furosemide
Pharmacology
Loop diuretic
Telemetry
ⓘ Educational reference — not clinical decision support
In short: Helps the body get rid of extra fluid by making the kidneys release more sodium and water — commonly used when fluid buildup makes it hard to breathe or puts strain on the heart.
Nursing implications: Monitor potassium and renal function, watch for orthostatic hypotension, track daily weights and I&Os, assess for ototoxicity with rapid IV push.
